Logo D: the arc is a live alert light

Shield and person stay white. Only the arc changes colour with the danger level where you are: green, amber or red. At red it also pulses slowly. It changes in the app’s top bar and in the browser tab icon.

DEMS

Where it can’t change: the phone home-screen icon is a fixed image, and phones don’t let a web app recolour it. There, and on the demo page and social previews, the arc is red, the brand colour. Everywhere live (top bar, browser tab), it shows your current level.
