C, rebuilt as an emergency service

Same dark + glass, now clearly an emergency app: a red SOS button on every screen (calls 1784, Thailand’s disaster hotline), a LIVE indicator, a 4-step danger scale with the standard safety colours, heavy bold headlines, and a sky glow that turns green, amber or red. At “Warning” the screen tells you exactly what to do.

Logo here: “Held”, now in signal red + white: a red arc of warning above, a white arc of support below, a person safe in the middle. Colours: brand = signal red #ff453a on near-black; danger levels = green / amber / orange / red, the international convention, so nobody has to learn them.
